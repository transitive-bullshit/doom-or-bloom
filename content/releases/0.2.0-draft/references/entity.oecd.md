## Neutral context

Intergovernmental AI principles and related policy work.

## Reported facts

The OECD AI Principles were adopted in May 2019 and updated in May 2024. They address rights, transparency, robustness, safety, accountability, and policy coordination.

## Interpretations

Shared principles may facilitate coordination. Endorsement alone does not measure compliance or reduce risk automatically.

## Known unknowns

Different jurisdictions may implement principles differently; principles are not a forecast of institutional success.

## Claim support

Supports claims that international AI policy standards exist. Does not establish binding worldwide enforcement or technical alignment.
