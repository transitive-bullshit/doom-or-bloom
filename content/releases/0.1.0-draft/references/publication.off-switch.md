## Neutral context

A formal model of incentives around a human-controlled off switch.

## Reported facts

Hadfield-Menell and colleagues analyze how uncertainty about utility and observing human actions can change an agent’s incentive to preserve an off switch under the model’s assumptions.

## Interpretations

The analysis illuminates an incentive problem and a possible design direction. A simplified game is not a demonstrated solution for deployed advanced systems.

## Known unknowns

Human rationality, utility uncertainty, and model assumptions limit transfer to real systems.

## Claim support

Supports reasoning about conditional shutdown incentives. Does not prove all agents resist shutdown or that objective uncertainty alone guarantees corrigibility.
