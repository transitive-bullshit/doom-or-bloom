## Neutral context

A study of empirical language-model scaling relationships.

## Reported facts

Kaplan and colleagues report power-law relationships between cross-entropy loss and model size, data, and training compute over studied ranges. They analyze allocation of a fixed compute budget.

## Interpretations

Regularities can support conditional engineering forecasts. Loss improvements are not identical to societal benefits or every downstream capability.

## Known unknowns

Extrapolation outside studied ranges, changing architectures, and task-specific performance remain uncertain.

## Claim support

Supports empirical scaling within the paper’s conditions. Does not prove infinite progress, a particular AGI date, or safe behavior.
