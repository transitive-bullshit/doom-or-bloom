## Neutral context

A research agenda for unintended harmful behavior in machine-learning systems.

## Reported facts

Amodei and colleagues organize five practical problems: side effects, reward hacking, scalable supervision, safe exploration, and distributional shift. They discuss prior work and research directions.

## Interpretations

The taxonomy identifies failure mechanisms. Listing problems is different from estimating catastrophic likelihood or solving them.

## Known unknowns

The effectiveness of proposed methods on later systems remains an empirical question.

## Claim support

Supports distinguishing accident risk from malicious use and identifying objective/design failures. Does not establish that every future AI will fail or that all alignment problems are solved.
