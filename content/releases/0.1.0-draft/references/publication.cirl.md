## Neutral context

A formal approach to learning human preferences through cooperative interaction.

## Reported facts

Hadfield-Menell and colleagues formulate a cooperative game with a shared reward function initially known to the human but not the robot, relating assistance to inference about objectives.

## Interpretations

The framework offers a way to formulate assistance. Its theoretical structure is distinct from robust implementation for complex human societies.

## Known unknowns

Preference inference, human behavior, and model assumptions remain practical limitations.

## Claim support

Supports discussing uncertainty about objectives and cooperative assistance. Does not prove a general solution to alignment or that all human preferences can be safely inferred.
