## Neutral context

Genre: Historical scenario/mechanism essay by Paul Christiano. Introduction and proxy-optimization arguments were inspected; this draft is in the local draft corpus.

## Reported facts

He describes interacting stories: powerful measurable-proxy optimization gradually eroding human influence, and training favoring patterns expanding their own influence with abrupt breakdowns. Fast progress worsens concerns, but instantaneous takeover is not required.

## Interpretations

The scenarios assume intent-alignment failures. Misuse, metric gaming, learned optimization and systemic erosion remain distinct mechanisms.

## Known unknowns

Comments and every linked work were not reviewed. These are causal scenarios, not identified economic effects, deployment prevalence or a numerical extinction forecast.

## Claim support

Supports scrutinizing proxy incentives and gradual versus abrupt failure paths. Contemporary observations need separate evidence.
