Genre: Argumentative threat-model essay by Eliezer Yudkowsky. This snapshot covers the preamble and selected opening arguments, in the local draft corpus.

## Reported facts

He argues alignment is extraordinarily difficult on expected development paths where an initial dangerous attempt may be irreversible and competition restricts preparation. The preamble allows sufficiently developed science could provide solutions.

## Interpretations

This practical, time-constrained pessimism differs from claiming alignment impossible in principle.
