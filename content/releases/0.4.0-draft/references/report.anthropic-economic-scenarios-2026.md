## Neutral context

Genre: Developer-associated economic research scenario explorer.

## Reported facts

A US task-based economic model explores conditional 2030 growth, employment, wages, and capital-share outcomes. Higher automation and faster adoption can produce larger growth with knowledge-worker displacement and uneven gains. Links a companion technical report and distinguishes scenarios from current-use measurement.

## Known unknowns

External reviewers provided criticism, not endorsement. Scenarios are not calibrated forecasts or established probabilities. Omits policy responses, business cycles, catastrophic risks, some demand/financial effects, and hyper-capable robotics; worker transitions are coarse. Survey respondents' implied scenarios measure expectations, not independent confirmation of future macroeconomic outcomes. Detailed modeling claims require the companion report.
