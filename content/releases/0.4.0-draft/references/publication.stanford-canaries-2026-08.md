## Neutral context

Genre: Descriptive working paper. Stanford researchers examine US payroll outcomes by age and occupational AI exposure; this revision is in the local draft corpus.

## Reported facts

Evidence through June 2026 uses balanced ADP-firm panels with 3.5–5 million employees/month, restricted to full-time positive-earnings workers under 70. The 19% headline is a kept-pace employment shortfall for ages 22–25 in the top two exposure quintiles relative to the bottom three, chiefly reflecting hiring.

## Interpretations

Occupational exposure proxies do not randomize or directly establish AI adoption. The headline differs from earlier controlled-regression estimates.

## Known unknowns

Pretrends, education attenuation and stronger patterns in ADP than national surveys limit attribution. Base compensation excludes bonuses. Payroll data are proprietary.

## Claim support

Supports dated descriptive hiring/employment differences. Does not establish national unemployment, an average causal AI effect or future displacement.
