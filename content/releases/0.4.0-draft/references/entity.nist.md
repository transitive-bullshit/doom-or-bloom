## Neutral context

NIST publishes AI risk-management guidance. Its AI RMF 1.0 is intended for voluntary use.

## Reported facts

The framework was released on January 26, 2023; the cited landing page describes a collaborative development process.

## Interpretations

Guidance can help structure risk work; its existence alone does not establish implementation or effectiveness.

## Known unknowns

## Claim support
