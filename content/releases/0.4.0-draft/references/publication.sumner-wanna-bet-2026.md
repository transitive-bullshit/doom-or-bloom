## Neutral context

Genre: Conceptual essay about AI risk and decision-making under uncertainty. Scott Sumner published it in The Pursuit of Happiness. Role: attributed reasoning perspective, not an empirical risk estimate.

## Reported facts

Sumner remains agnostic about AI extinction risk and specific policies. He considers an expert-survey median a provisional starting point, not his own endorsed probability. He distinguishes technical expertise from forecasting skill, inside from outside views, and good decisions from fortunate outcomes. His tradeoff discussion includes transformative benefits, unequal circumstances, future generations, and risks AI might both create and reduce.
