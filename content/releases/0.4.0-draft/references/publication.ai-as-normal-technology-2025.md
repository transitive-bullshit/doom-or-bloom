## Neutral context

Genre: Worldview/policy paper by Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor. This draft covers the introduction and selected adoption arguments, in the local draft corpus.

## Reported facts

The authors distinguish advances in methods, applications and adoption. They predict substantial institutionally mediated change over decades, advocate resilience/uncertainty reduction and argue people/organizations can retain control.

## Interpretations

They distinguish description, prediction and prescription, presenting an unquantified median scenario rather than measured inevitability.

## Known unknowns

Historical analogies and benchmark critiques may change in applicability. Not every citation was independently checked; slow adoption does not imply all harms are small.

## Claim support

Supports examining adoption constraints and institutional-control assumptions. Does not prove that capabilities must progress slowly or negate severe risk.

## Related entries

Situational Awareness has different acceleration assumptions; an AGI ontology separates capability dimensions from adoption.
