## Neutral context

Genre: Theoretical social-epistemology paper and normative argument by Nick Bostrom, Thomas Douglas and Anders Sandberg.

## Reported facts

Indexed primary excerpts analyze group bias when any one altruistic actor can initiate a consequential action using a fallible value estimate. They propose deliberation/deference and discuss harms from excessive conformity.

## Interpretations

The conclusion depends on decision structure and assumptions. It is not a blanket rule to obey authority or silence dissent.
