## Neutral context

A research nonprofit evaluating frontier-model capabilities and risks.

## Reported facts

METR describes evaluations of autonomous task performance and research on developer productivity, evaluation integrity, and risk mitigation. Its mission emphasizes scientific assessment of catastrophic risks. The team announced its move from ARC Evals to the METR name in December 2023.

## Interpretations

Task evaluations may inform forecasts, but benchmark scope and deployment conditions affect how results transfer.

Evaluation coverage, generalization, and the capabilities of later systems cannot be assumed from the organization’s existence.
