## Neutral context

NIST AI 100-1 is the 2023 voluntary framework for managing risks associated with AI systems.

## Reported facts

Its core functions are Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage.

## Interpretations

It provides a management structure; it does not certify that any particular system is safe.

## Known unknowns

Implementation, measurement quality, and future applicability vary by context.

## Claim support

Supports: the framework’s management functions. Does not establish: mandatory law or resolution of catastrophic risk.
