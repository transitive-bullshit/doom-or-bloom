## Neutral context

Genre: Developer risk-governance policy. Targeted provisions of v3.4 are drafted here, in the local draft corpus.

## Reported facts

Thresholds address chemical-biological weapons, high-stakes misalignment and automated R&D. The policy distinguishes company commitments from industry recommendations and revisable Roadmap goals. Appendix A includes competitor-contingent delays.

## Interpretations

Written thresholds and safeguards describe a decision framework, rather than observed compliance or mitigation adequacy.

## Known unknowns

CEO/RSO assessment approval, additional Board/LTBT approval for major marginal-risk reliance and specified external-review triggers have conditions. Reviews allow indicated legal/IP/safety/privacy redactions; annual compliance review concerns procedures. Other risks and regulatory obligations are not comprehensively covered.

## Claim support

Supports identifying selected current written provisions and qualifications. Does not establish unconditional pauses, complete transparency or independent safety assurance.

## Related entries

Developer card disclosures contain separate model-specific evidence, not automatic proof of policy compliance.
