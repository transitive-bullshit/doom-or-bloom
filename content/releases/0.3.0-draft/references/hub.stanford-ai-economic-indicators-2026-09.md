## Neutral context

Genre: Mutable measurement hub. The Digital Economy Lab organizes employment, adoption, macroeconomic and consumer-surplus indicators. This scoped draft is in the local draft corpus.

## Reported facts

The inspected Canaries methodology uses occupation-coded workers in a rolling five-year balanced ADP-firm sample. Exposure quintiles weight occupations equally, not workers. July GDP-B valuations measure users’ willingness to accept compensation, not GDP.

## Interpretations

Payroll exposure relationships are observational; different series have different populations and evidence windows.

## Known unknowns

Balanced firms exclude entry, exit and payroll-provider changes. Neither all US workers nor all ADP clients are represented. Every child dashboard and raw download was not audited.

## Claim support

Ground a named indicator’s definition and vintage. Neither observed hiring nor consumer valuation alone establishes causal displacement or macroeconomic transformation.

## Related entries

Canaries is underlying research, not independent corroboration. Danish earnings research has different outcomes and samples.
