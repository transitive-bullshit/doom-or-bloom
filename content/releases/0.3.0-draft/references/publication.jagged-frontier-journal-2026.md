## Neutral context

Genre: Randomized workplace experiment, accessible related journal version. The required HBS 24-013 working-paper copy remains partly unread; these methods belong to the journal version.

## Reported facts

A preregistered study randomized 758 BCG consultants into task arms and no-AI, GPT-4 or GPT-4-plus-prompting conditions. The tool used end-April 2023 GPT-4. Assigned AI improved inside-frontier performance; the discussion reports a 19-percentage-point correctness reduction on one outside-frontier case.

## Interpretations

Random assignment identifies treatment effects in these tasks; better presentation need not imply correctness.

## Known unknowns

Selected consultants/tasks, subjective grading, incentives and one outside case limit transfer. Exact collection dates and required-copy byte identity were not established.

## Claim support

Ground task-dependent assistance effects. Does not identify September 2026 model boundaries, economy-wide employment effects or a universal 19% performance penalty.
