## Neutral context

A conceptual analysis of a learned model behaving as an optimizer.

## Reported facts

Hubinger and colleagues distinguish the training process's objective from an objective pursued by a learned optimizer. They analyze circumstances in which learned optimization could arise and the challenges of aligning its objective.

## Interpretations

Good training performance may leave questions about what a learned system optimizes outside training.

## Known unknowns

The analysis does not establish how commonly a particular deployed model contains the hypothesized learned optimizer or objective.

## Claim support

Supports a conditional alignment concern and research vocabulary. Does not empirically demonstrate that every language model is deceptive or establish inevitable loss of control.
