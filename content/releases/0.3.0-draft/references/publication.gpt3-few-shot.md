## Neutral context

The GPT-3 paper investigates language-model performance with examples in the prompt.

## Reported facts

Brown and colleagues evaluate an autoregressive language model on tasks in zero-, one-, and few-shot settings without task-specific gradient updates for the reported evaluations.

## Interpretations

The work demonstrates useful task adaptation in context. Task coverage, benchmark design, and limitations qualify extrapolation.

## Known unknowns

Prompted performance does not itself establish robust autonomy, alignment, or general real-world reliability.

## Claim support

Supports claims about reported few-shot language-model capabilities. Does not establish that the model learns every task reliably or that training-data concerns disappear.
