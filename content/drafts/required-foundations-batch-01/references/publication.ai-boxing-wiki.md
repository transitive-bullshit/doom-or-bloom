## Neutral context

Genre: Conceptual containment wiki. The LessWrong entry discusses restricting a powerful AI’s interaction with the world.

## Reported facts

It separates technical isolation from human persuasion risks, reports human roleplay experiments and surveys proposed strategies. Some escape paths are speculative.

## Interpretations

Human persuasion games are not demonstrations of an actual superintelligence escaping a secure computer.

## Known unknowns

Skepticism about lasting containment is a position, not a quantified universal impossibility result. Actual permissions and environments matter.

## Claim support

Supports distinguishing conceptual containment, persuasion and operational isolation. Current sandbox vulnerabilities or self-replication require separately dated evidence.

## Related entries

Treacherous Turn concerns concealed goals/opportunity, rather than proof of technical escape.
