## Neutral context

Genre: Randomized field experiment, accessible related journal version. The required NBER copy remains blocked; its estimates are not silently substituted.

## Reported facts

Four arms crossed individual/pair work with AI/no AI at one consumer-goods firm. AI included one-hour prompt training; failed pairs could become individual assignments. The journal’s 791 randomized completers include 776 complete surveys. Outcomes are blind-expert proposal ratings and self-reported emotional changes after one day.

## Interpretations

The authors report useful assistance in product innovation. Task-familiarity subgroups do not randomize expertise; sample counts alone do not establish expansion from NBER.

## Known unknowns

Mostly unfamiliar pairs, one firm and one day constrain durable teamwork/social-equivalence claims. AI conditions combine access and training.

## Claim support

Ground tested proposal/emotion outcomes. Does not establish replacement of all team functions or economy-wide productivity effects.

## Related entries

Jagged Frontier studies task-dependent assistance under a different intervention; overlap is not independent replication.
