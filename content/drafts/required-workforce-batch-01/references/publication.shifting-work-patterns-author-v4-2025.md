## Neutral context

Genre: Randomized field study, accessible author revision. The exact required NBER copy remains blocked; this snapshot pins arXiv v4.

## Reported facts

The study allocated Microsoft 365 Copilot access within firm-recruited knowledge-worker lists. Main outcomes cover rollout months 4–6, with some firms ending earlier. The revised weekly email-time estimates distinguish 1.4-hour intent-to-treat from 2-hour IV/LATE effects.

## Interpretations

ITT concerns assignment; IV/LATE uses assignment to instrument use. Neither is simply a voluntary frequent-user comparison.

## Known unknowns

Telemetry measures application activity, not overall paid hours, output quality or productivity. Nonrepresentative recruitment, app exclusions, existing AI tools and limited coworker licensing constrain transfer and spillover claims.

## Claim support

Supports task/application behavior changes under the tested intervention. Does not establish aggregate GDP, profit or verified output-quality gains.
