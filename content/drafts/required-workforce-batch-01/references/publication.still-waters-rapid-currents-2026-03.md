## Neutral context

Genre: Administrative-data quasi-experiment, accessible author revision. Exact NBER full text remains blocked; its previous title is retained as an alias.

## Reported facts

Danish surveys in 11 exposed occupations link to earnings/hours registries through December 2024. The revised abstract reports null effects ruling out changes above 2% two years after ChatGPT’s launch, alongside task restructuring. Adoption and employer encouragement are observed, not randomized.

## Interpretations

Difference-in-differences relies on counterfactual trends. Adopter-relative effects difference out common changes; spillover inference needs further assumptions.

## Known unknowns

Self-reported time savings differ from administrative hours. The 12-percentage-point occupation-switcher earnings comparison is descriptive subgroup growth, not an average causal effect. Country, occupations and early-model vintage constrain transfer.

## Claim support

Ground dated registry outcomes under the identification assumptions. Null estimates do not eliminate future displacement, task benefits or distributional change.
