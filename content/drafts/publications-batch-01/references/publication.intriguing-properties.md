## Neutral context

An early empirical study of neural-network representations and adversarial image inputs.

## Reported facts

Szegedy and colleagues report that small, deliberately selected image perturbations can cause confident misclassification. Some adversarial inputs transfer between networks. They also examine whether semantic information belongs to individual units or combinations of units.

## Interpretations

High task accuracy can coexist with vulnerabilities outside familiar evaluation conditions.

## Known unknowns

The experiments do not establish the prevalence or consequences of comparable failures in every architecture or deployment.

## Claim support

Supports a bounded claim about adversarial vulnerability and evaluation limits. Does not establish that every AI system is unsafe or assign a catastrophic-risk probability.
