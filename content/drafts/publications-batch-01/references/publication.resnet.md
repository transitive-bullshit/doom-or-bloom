## Neutral context

An image-recognition paper introducing a residual learning formulation for very deep networks.

## Reported facts

He and colleagues describe networks that learn residual functions relative to layer inputs. They report easier optimization, a 152-layer ImageNet model, a winning 2015 classification ensemble and improvements on detection tasks.

## Interpretations

An architectural change can improve trainability and task performance without itself settling general intelligence.

## Known unknowns

Transfer from the evaluated vision tasks to other domains depends on additional evidence.
