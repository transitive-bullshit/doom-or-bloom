## Neutral context

An assistant-training method guided by an authored set of principles and model-produced feedback.

## Reported facts

The authors describe a supervised phase using self-critiques and revised responses, followed by reinforcement learning using preferences evaluated by an AI model. They report a less evasive assistant capable of explaining objections to harmful requests.

## Interpretations

Authored principles can structure feedback while reducing the need for some human labels.

## Known unknowns

The results depend on the selected principles, evaluation conditions and feedback models; broader reliability needs separate assessment.

## Claim support

Supports a particular training approach and reported behavioral improvements. Does not remove human choices about values or guarantee safe behavior in every situation.
