## Neutral context

A language-representation paper using bidirectional Transformer pretraining.

## Reported facts

Devlin and colleagues pretrain representations using context from both directions. They describe fine-tuning with an added output layer and report improved results across eleven language-processing tasks.

## Interpretations

Reusable pretraining can improve multiple downstream tasks with comparatively small task-specific additions.

## Known unknowns

The evaluated tasks do not establish reliability for arbitrary claims or applications outside that distribution.

## Claim support

Supports a bidirectional representation-learning result. BERT is not, by this result alone, an autoregressive conversational assistant or a generally reliable source of truth.
