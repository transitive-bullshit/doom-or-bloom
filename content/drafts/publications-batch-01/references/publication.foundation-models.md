## Neutral context

An interdisciplinary report examining widely pretrained models and their downstream use.

## Reported facts

Bommasani and colleagues describe foundation models as broadly trained models adaptable to many tasks. The report surveys capabilities, technical foundations, applications and societal effects. It highlights how downstream systems can inherit shared model defects.

## Interpretations

Reuse creates opportunities and correlated weaknesses whose assessment can require more than a single discipline.

## Known unknowns

The report frames unresolved capability and failure questions rather than supplying a universal method for evaluating them.

## Claim support

Supports sociotechnical analysis of broad pretraining and downstream dependence. Does not imply that every adapted model has identical behavior or that opportunities guarantee beneficial outcomes.
