## Neutral context

A cross-disciplinary report examining malicious uses and possible responses.

## Reported facts

Brundage and colleagues survey digital, physical and political security threats. They propose recommendations involving policy, responsible practice, research and collaboration to prepare for misuse.

## Interpretations

Misuse can change attack methods and incentives, while prevention also depends on institutions and defensive capabilities.

## Known unknowns

A forecast of possible threats does not resolve their frequency, severity or the eventual balance between attackers and defenders.

## Claim support

Supports distinguishing misuse pathways and mitigation agendas. Does not document a single realized catastrophe or show that every forecasted attack is inevitable.
