## Neutral context

A study of why models can be vulnerable to adversarial perturbations and how adversarial examples can aid training.

## Reported facts

Goodfellow, Shlens and Szegedy propose a linear explanation for adversarial vulnerability and a fast method for generating adversarial examples. They report an adversarial-training improvement for a maxout network on MNIST.

## Interpretations

A vulnerability can motivate a training intervention, but the intervention's scope matters.

## Known unknowns

The reported improvement does not establish resistance to all attacks, distributions or model types.

## Claim support

Supports a specific attack-generation and training result. Does not prove that adversarial training guarantees comprehensive safety or that one benchmark captures all relevant robustness.
