## Neutral context

A dataset-documentation proposal.

## Reported facts

Gebru and colleagues propose accompanying datasets with documentation of motivation, composition, collection, recommended uses, and related characteristics. The framework aims to improve communication between creators and users and encourage transparency and accountability. The record notes subsequent publication in Communications of the ACM.

## Interpretations

Provenance information can help users assess dataset suitability.

## Known unknowns

A documentation proposal does not itself demonstrate that every disclosure is complete, accurate, or sufficient to prevent downstream harms.

## Claim support

Supports dataset provenance and suitability documentation as an accountability practice. Does not prove documentation alone removes bias, makes a dataset lawful, or guarantees safety.
