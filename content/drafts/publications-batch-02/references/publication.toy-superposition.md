## Neutral context

A simplified interpretability study of overlapping feature representations.

## Reported facts

Elhage and colleagues study simplified networks that represent sparse features in superposition. Their toy setting explains how unrelated features can share neurons, exhibits a phase transition and geometric structure, and supplies evidence connecting superposition to adversarial examples.

## Interpretations

Shared neurons can complicate reading representations one unit at a time.

## Known unknowns

A deliberately simplified model does not fully characterize deployed language models or establish a universal mechanism.

## Claim support

Supports superposition as a tractable explanation of some polysemantic representations and an interpretability challenge. Does not demonstrate a complete understanding of frontier models, or prove interpretability is impossible.
