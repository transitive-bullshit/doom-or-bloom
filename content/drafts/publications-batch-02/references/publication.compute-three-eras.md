## Neutral context

A historical analysis of model-training compute.

## Reported facts

Sevilla and colleagues analyze historical training-compute trends, distinguishing pre-deep-learning, deep-learning, and large-scale eras. They report approximate doubling times of 20 months before 2010 and six months following early-2010s deep learning, alongside a separate large-scale trend emerging in late 2015.

## Interpretations

Historical growth is evidence about a period, not a guaranteed extrapolation.

## Known unknowns

Historical selected-system trends depend on data and era definitions; compute is only one input alongside data and algorithms.

## Claim support

Supports rapid historical growth in training compute. Does not guarantee indefinite extrapolation, establish a current doubling time, or determine an AGI arrival date.
