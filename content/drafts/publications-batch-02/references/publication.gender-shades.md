## Neutral context

A study of intersectional classifier-performance disparities.

## Reported facts

Buolamwini and Gebru analyze skin-type and gender representation in facial-analysis datasets, introduce a balanced evaluation dataset, and test three commercial gender classifiers. Reported error reaches 34.7% for darker-skinned females, compared with a maximum 0.8% for lighter-skinned males.

## Interpretations

Aggregate accuracy can obscure consequential subgroup differences.

## Known unknowns

Results concern binary gender classification, selected systems, and the study's group definitions; they do not describe every facial-recognition task or later model.

## Claim support

Supports substantial intersectional performance disparities in these systems. Does not prove identical disparities across every AI system or establish current vendor performance.
