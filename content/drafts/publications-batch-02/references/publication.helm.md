## Neutral context

A framework for evaluating several model qualities together.

## Reported facts

Liang and colleagues introduce HELM's scenario taxonomy and standardized multi-metric evaluation. Their reported study evaluates 30 language models over 42 scenarios, including accuracy, calibration, robustness, fairness, bias, toxicity, and efficiency where feasible. It exposes tradeoffs and releases prompts, completions, and a modular toolkit.

## Interpretations

Evaluation tradeoffs can be missed by one headline accuracy result.

## Known unknowns

Selected scenarios omit some populations and desiderata; the publication's model set is historical, while HELM continues evolving.

## Claim support

Supports broad, comparable evaluation beyond accuracy. Does not establish a complete safety certificate or a permanent ranking of current models.
