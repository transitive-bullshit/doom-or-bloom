## Neutral context

A model-documentation and evaluation-reporting proposal.

## Reported facts

Mitchell and colleagues propose short documents describing intended model uses, evaluation methods, and performance under relevant demographic and intersectional conditions. They illustrate the framework with smiling-face and toxic-comment classifiers, aiming to make limitations more visible to users.

## Interpretations

Disclosure can help users assess intended uses and evaluation gaps.

## Known unknowns

Documentation quality and evaluation coverage vary; disclosure does not by itself correct poor performance or govern use.

## Claim support

Supports model-specific transparency and disaggregated evaluation. Does not establish that a documented model is fair, safe, or suitable for any unspecified high-stakes application.
