## Neutral context

Genre: Research preprint and proof-of-concept experiments.

## Reported facts

Demonstrates an open-weight-model worm adapting exploitation and replicating across a heterogeneous 33-machine simulated corporate network. Evaluates additional inference-service deployment separately.

## Interpretations

Claims are attributed to the source within its scope.

## Known unknowns

Independent proof-of-concept capability evidence, not corroboration of the OpenAI incident or a detected wild outbreak. Every target was deliberately vulnerable; active endpoint defenses were absent. Main propagation experiments used shared inference infrastructure, potentially optimistic for timing. Reported zero marginal infection cost assumes stolen compute and excludes broader attack costs. Results do not establish reliable spread through hardened networks or autonomous real-world persistence.
