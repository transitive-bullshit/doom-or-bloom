## Assessment refinements

- Explicitly unknown worldview positions remain unplaced even when the uncertainty itself is clearly stated. Epistemic reasoning may still be assessed; missing expected outcomes never become low directional scores.
- The five-part fingerprint is timeline, expected upside, catastrophic risk, technical controllability, and institutional competence. Timing is an exact excerpt from the participant, with unknown timing shown explicitly. Catastrophe is evaluated separately from ordinary harm and has no extra horizontal-map weight.
- Catastrophic-risk categories run from expected negligible/avoided outcomes through limited possibility and material possibility to catastrophe dominating the outlook. General harm and safeguards preferences do not establish catastrophic risk. The narrow Jev question templates are also reviewable in [questions.json](../content/rubrics/0.1.0-draft/questions.json). The full category criteria are in [rubric.json](../content/rubrics/0.1.0-draft/rubric.json).
- An early missing horizon receives a one-point authored routing preference before the third answer. Material ambiguity can still win. The result unlock remains three substantive answers, independent of answering a timing prompt.
- Domain familiarity selects wording and resources only. Credentials, namedropping and jargon alone cannot establish expert familiarity; familiarity has no quality-score weight.
- Exact answer excerpts and primary-source links appear in evidence and the report. Rejected interaction history is bounded to the latest 20 entries on the device and excluded from later requests and report text.

Examples: “AI will eliminate paperwork but I have no idea about humanity-scale catastrophe” can express ordinary benefit while catastrophic risk remains unplaced. “Severe collapse is a material possibility if oversight fails” expresses a conditional catastrophic-risk position. “The robot overlords arrive Tuesday—seriously, I don’t know the timing” remains usable humor with uncertain timing, not a clear miss.
