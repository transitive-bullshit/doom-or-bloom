## Claim boundaries

- Plans, ontologies, research programs, practitioner guidance, scenarios and observed capability are distinct content types. Their existence does not establish effective mitigation or deployment safety.
- RSP v3.4 has conditional delays, governance approvals, external-review triggers and permitted redactions. Industry recommendations, company commitments and revisable goals remain distinct. Procedure review is not outcome validation; actual compliance was not assessed.
- Four OECD scenario classes have no assigned probabilities. Ontology dimensions and future pathways do not place a contemporary model without separately verified evaluation evidence.
- Control separates ability to subvert safeguards from propensity. It depends on threat models, trusted components and environment; a definition is not complete containment assurance.
- The NBER volume and National Academies records retain partial access. Indexed remit/summary cannot justify unread chapter findings, evidence tables or universal scientific efficacy. The NBER publication/citation discrepancy remains unresolved.
- Edition/publication dates do not refresh older inputs or interventions. These sources complement the recent incident/evaluation corpus without becoming current quantitative performance estimates.
- Related entries are conceptual or discovery relationships, not independent corroboration. No source affiliation or agreement with a thesis changes reasoning scores.
