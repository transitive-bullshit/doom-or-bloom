## Core interpretation

Strongly concerned about a near-term, civilization-scale transition, with a specific mechanism: coding automation accelerates research and then capabilities. Distinguish automated coding from fully automated AI research and from superintelligence. His latest interview gives end-2028 as the median for fully automated research, not a certainty. He favors deliberate pacing and verified transparency, not passive doom fatalism.
