## Access limitations and exclusions

- Liang: [Tencent Technology’s original WeChat publication](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Rqv-4yFGUTuLxMaRSt1XDg) could not be opened. A [Sina republication](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/companyt/2026-07-23/doc-iniiufvk4580366.shtml) exposed indexed excerpts but the opened page returned not found. We inspected [selected translated Q&A](https://thelowdown.momentum.asia/full-transcript-of-deepseek-founder-liang-wenfengs-fundraising-meeting/) about continual learning and research assistance. The publisher explicitly describes a circulating document claiming to transcribe the meeting. The source and every belief derived from it are excluded from the executable persona. No assertion that the full meeting or its recording was authenticated.
- Sutton: searches surfaced recirculated older interviews and profiles. No crawl date was used as a publication date.
- Ilya: NVIDIA’s announcement is an interested party’s description. Public scale-up confidence is useful grounding; it does not validate alignment or reveal a secret research technique.
- Fei-Fei: a company product page or another speaker’s answer is not automatically evidence of her personal worldview. The brief favors explicit first-person material.
- Russell and Tegmark: retain a strong voice while avoiding false precision, unsourced probability estimates, sensational incident details and unreliable historical analogies.

## Intended distinctions to inspect after live generation

These are qualitative review questions, not instructions to Jev or expected scores:

- Can the system represent Bengio’s alternative technical path while preserving his strong concern?
- Can it separate Sutskever’s criticism of present recipes from confidence in profound eventual transformation?
- Does Karpathy’s bounded automation remain distinct from an assertion of solved recursive self-improvement?
- Does Li retain an ambitious spatial and human-centered vision rather than becoming a generic moderate?
- Does Sutton’s positive valuation of succession remain distinct from confidence in continued human control?
- Do Russell and Tegmark retain sharp safety demands without losing their interest in useful AI?
- Does Liang remain research-ambitious without the participant inventing a comprehensive safety position to fill gaps?
