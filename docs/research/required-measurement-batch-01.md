## Reading and evidence boundaries

- Twelve previously unmapped required URLs now have scoped draft mappings. The three developer hubs retain their original URLs and map to both an index snapshot and a separately pinned child card. The child is related evidence, not a substitute for the required hub or evidence that all its documents were read.
- September model cards are recent developer disclosures. External test names do not confer independent overall safety assurance. Astra’s September 9 revision, Anthropic’s inconsistent Fable/Mythos labeling and Gemini 3.8’s inherited 3.7 frontier assessment remain explicit.
- METR TH 1.1 measures human-expert task time at a selected success rate, rather than runtime or reliable worker replacement. Its May vintage is not automatically a September frontier measurement. The risk pilot covers February–March, rather than later incidents.
- Annual report editions and dashboard access dates do not replace evidence windows. The AI Index includes 2025 series; AISI’s first report covers through October 2025. International synthesis does not establish consensus numerical catastrophe probabilities.
- Epoch’s scaling hub is a discovery record; individual child results require separate methods/version review. Its 2030 forecast remains a dated conditional argument with a disclosed DeepMind commission. Organizational self-disclosures are context, not proof of bias or independence.
- Only the scope recorded in the research notes is authored. Full chapters, every dashboard source and every linked evaluation were not comprehensively inspected. These records cannot support claims beyond their inspected summary/method/limitations passages.
- Cross-links capture dependencies or shared topics; they never add independent corroboration or an ideology-based routing rule. No asset is activated, individually approved or frozen by this batch.
