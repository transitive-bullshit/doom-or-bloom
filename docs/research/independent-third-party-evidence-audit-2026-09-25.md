## Next evidence priorities and limits

- For interview-heavy figures such as Simon Willison, Jeremy Howard, Omar Khattab and Erik Bernhardsson, search publisher transcripts and author-linked talks around mechanisms missing from their briefs. Their authored essays already provide substantive evidence; absence of interview URLs alone does not show inaccurate beliefs.
- For the X-only briefs and pseudonymous accounts, prioritize longer original essays or direct interviews where identity is established. Do not invent biographies or infer policy from affiliation. Some users may legitimately have no accessible long-form interview.
- Preserve older foundational arguments alongside recent revisions. Do not let a recent short post erase an earlier detailed view without actual contradictory evidence.
- Source briefs and frozen generated answers are separate artifacts. Adding sources cannot establish that the saved result used them. Reruns should cite the new snapshot and elicit unresolved dimensions without setting a desired map position.
- Preview completeness is independent of substantive research completeness: a thumbnail, favicon or description proves neither transcript inspection nor correct attribution.
