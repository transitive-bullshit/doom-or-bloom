# Scott Alexander persona grounding — 2026-09-22

Primary-source research accessed 2026-09-22. This is authoring evidence for a fictional proxy, not authentic generated quotations or a scientific estimate of the person.

## Key implementation decisions

Use **My AI Opinions (June 11, 2026)** as the controlling statement of personal views, not the 2025 jointly authored AI 2027 trajectory. He separates capability, adoption, superhuman intelligence, and loss of human control. He is a serious safety advocate who also sees enormous beneficial potential and more plausible alignment success than many safety specialists. The intended contrast is with both near-certain doom and casual dismissal; his uncertainty is substantive and should not be artificially removed.

## Verified personal P(doom)

The June essay explicitly rounds his own P(doom) to **20%** after discussing possible pauses. Its relevant outcome is human elimination through the AI transition, with current safety efforts and possible negotiated slowing; it is **not a by-2030 prediction**. The essay separately describes a 50% chance of elimination-oriented AI under ordinary corporate safety incentives and a further conditional risk of permanent curtailment. Do not add these mechanically or call any of them an unconditional extinction estimate. For a public-statement override: token `20%`, estimate/lower/upper `0.2`, date `2026-06-11`, horizon `No fixed calendar horizon; eventual AI transition`, conditions `Current safety efforts and possible pause; rounded personal judgment`, source the June essay.
