## Andrew McAfee on AI, jobs, and ‘permissionless innovation’

- Publisher: McKinsey Global Institute, Century of Plenty podcast
- Published: 2026-09-15
- URL: https://www.mckinsey.com/mgi/our-research/a-century-of-plenty/podcast/andrew-mcafee-on-ai-jobs-and-permissionless-innovation
- Access: original publisher's edited interview transcript, read in full
- Suggested source kind: interview

McAfee says AI advances exceeded his expectations and favors permissionless innovation over preemptive steering. He revised earlier unemployment worries after sustained employment and expects disruption without mass technological unemployment. He nevertheless recognizes damaging local job losses and apprenticeship problems that merit responses now. He regards rising prosperity and lower resource intensity as compatible, while explicitly supporting government action against demonstrated pollution externalities. This is opposition to anticipatory restrictions, not a rejection of every regulation.

## Why I Didn't Sign the AI Open Letter

- Publisher: Andrew McAfee, The Geek Way
- Published: 2026-07-13
- URL: https://geekway.substack.com/p/why-i-didnt-sign-the-ai-open-letter
- Access: complete authored article
- Suggested source kind: essay

McAfee expects substantially stronger AI within ten years and considers major economic transformation likely, but doubts an unprecedented transformation by 2030. He objects to emphasizing risks first and to building institutions to steer innovation before impacts are understood. His alternative calls for quickly responding to emerging harms while retaining economic freedom. He acknowledges new risks and disruptions alongside improved living standards. The article directly distinguishes rapid technical improvement from uncertain economic diffusion.

## The future of innovation, with Andrew McAfee

- Publisher: Niskanen Center, The Permanent Problem podcast
- Published: 2026-03-26
- URL: https://www.niskanencenter.org/the-future-of-innovation-with-andrew-mcafee/
- Access: original publisher's transcript; AI discussion and subsequent economic-policy discussion read
- Suggested source kind: interview

McAfee sees powerful, still-improving AI but withholds a firm judgment on whether LLMs alone reach AGI. He welcomes alternative approaches. Organizational inertia and protections for incumbents slow adoption. Automating tasks does not mechanically eliminate jobs; nevertheless, he recognizes early hiring weakness and possible employment declines in particular industries. He favors experimentation over preserving existing jobs by restricting innovation. His remarks express uncertainty about the scale of disruption, not certainty of painless adjustment.

## Why Andrew McAfee thinks AI can save the planet

- Publisher: Latitude Studios, Where the Internet Lives, in partnership with Google
- Published: 2026-01-12
- URL: https://www.latitudemedia.com/news/wtil-why-andrew-mcafee-thinks-ai-can-save-the-planet/
- Access: complete original interview-derived article; not the full podcast transcript
- Suggested source kind: interview

McAfee expects AI to transform many sectors within a decade and accelerate the energy transition. He argues that efficiency improvements and scientific applications can outweigh data-center resource consumption, with commercial incentives driving more efficient infrastructure. This supports strong environmental and economic upside expectations, rather than a numerical extinction-risk estimate. The publisher explicitly labels this Google-sponsored partner content, a relevant provenance caveat.
