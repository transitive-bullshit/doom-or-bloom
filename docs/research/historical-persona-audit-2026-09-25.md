# Historical evidence and simulation fidelity audit — September 25, 2026

This pass covers only the 97 Independent additions. It follows the Robert Miles review and investigates whether sparse evidence or lossy summaries made other simulations look more neutral than their expressed positions.

## Findings

The pre-audit collection had 26 of 97 outlooks between 45 and 55, including 18 between 49 and 51. A midpoint alone is not an error. In the inspected cases, the assessment generally reflected the generated answer: the loss of fidelity happened earlier, when a source summary omitted an expectation or the participant converted a research limitation into personal uncertainty. The reverse also occurred: enthusiasm for engineering became unsupported optimism about society.

- **Connor Leahy:** the old brief covered only the opening of a long interview. The [publisher transcript](https://www.thegreatsimplification.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/TGS-184-Connor-Leahy-Transcript.pdf) includes a 50–80% extinction-or-near-extinction estimate over 50 years if humanity literally does nothing. [Senate testimony](https://sencanada.ca/en/content/sen/committee/451/trcm/28ev-57613-e) explicitly predicts loss of control and likely extinction if superintelligence is built. Preserve the conditions and horizon; this is not an unconditional probability override.
- **Zvi Mowshowitz:** his [March 2026 interview](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/zvi-s-mic-works-recursive-self-improvement-live-player-analysis-anthropic-vs-dow-more/) retains roughly 70% p(doom) while recognizing promising alignment work. The previous brief omitted that personal expectation.
- **Roko:** his own [February discussion](https://lironshapira.substack.com/p/q-and-a-february-2026) argues that alignment is comparatively tractable and likely to improve. His concern about human conflict remains distinct. The old summary lost this distinction even though the URL was already present.
- **Jessica Taylor:** her [alignment essay](https://unstableontology.com/2023/12/31/a-case-for-ai-alignment-being-difficult/) describes harmful default mechanisms and potential countermeasures. Missing information about her overall forecast did not justify inventing personal agnosticism.
- **Other material gaps:** historical risk arguments from anthrupad and Bone; a misframed Seconds air-gap argument; Nick Dobos's underrepresented swarm warning; missing open-science and governance positions from samsja and willcb; and unsupported broad optimism in Vogel's generated answer.

## Coverage limits

All 97 received source/answer coverage triage, but depth varied. The first and last batches included bounded historical X searches; the last batch also inspected all 178 existing X URLs. The middle batch concentrated deep primary inspection on 11 material corrections. No unchanged user is certified exhaustively audited. Unavailable interviews remain leads, and sparse historical material is not proof that a person holds no view. Engagement prioritizes discovery; it does not establish representativeness or truth. Historical opinions do not automatically override newer explicit revisions.

The 53 unchanged users retain their selected results. Remaining priorities include John Scott-Railton's long-form surveillance interview, inaccessible or metadata-only interview leads, and sparse pseudonymous accounts where generated dialogue must be separated from human statements.
