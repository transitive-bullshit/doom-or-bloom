## Attribution and assessment boundaries

- API authorship/timestamps and complete root text establish what was posted, rather than independent truth of employment, institutional practices, benchmark results, incident attribution or commercial promises. Threads, replies and images were not inspected.
- Edited roots and quote-posts retain their dependencies. Repetition does not multiply independent evidence; captions are not chart reconstructions or controlled evaluations.
- Forecasts of neocloud replication and agent ecologies are conditional proposals. Preserve Achiam's uncertainty and distinction between degrees of control loss and catastrophe rather than forcing a high-risk interpretation.
- Reported vulnerability counts differ from discovery, exploitation and attack counts. No AI-causal trend is established. Diver's national-supply qualification and absence of an AI claim remain explicit.
- Official transcript export does not mean full-video review. Caption errors and missing speaker labels constrain attribution; publication dates do not establish recording dates. Publisher titles cannot establish forecast endpoints, whistleblower status or consensus.
- Advocacy and incident retellings do not become forensic investigations. Exact technical claims require their original empirical source before factual scoring beyond the obtained attribution scope.
