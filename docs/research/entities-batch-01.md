# Entities research batch 01

Twenty agent research drafts, **not human reviewed**. Access date is 2026-09-17. Each record distinguishes first-party reports from our interpretation; organizational mission statements do not independently establish effectiveness or impartiality. Snapshot dates bound the claims rather than asserting every source was updated that day. University centers and corporate teams are scoped units, not additional independent parent organizations. Full titles are acceptable aliases; the additional aliases below avoid generic words and ambiguous acronyms.

## 1. Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence

- Suggested ID: `entity.stanford-hai`
- Aliases: `Stanford HAI`; `Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI`.
- Snapshot: 2026-09-17. Primary URL / access: [HAI about](https://hai.stanford.edu/about), 2026-09-17.
