# Core persona source refresh — 2026-09-21

## Scope and method

Reviewed and expanded the ten editorial persona briefs in `public-personas.ts` and `additional-public-personas.ts`. Research used primary essays, speaker-attributed interview transcripts, official research abstracts, and clearly scoped publisher descriptions. Existing strong stress-test voices remain intact. No target judgments were added. Saved journey snapshots and generated answers were not changed; these sources become participant context on a future regeneration.

Checked source bodies or indexed primary article text, rather than treating search crawl dates as publication dates. Older sources are retained for specific mechanisms or voice. This refresh adds coverage rather than claiming each link is an independent piece of corroborating evidence.

## Interpretation changes

- Jensen remains the deliberately incendiary anti-doomer proxy. Added adoption, open-model defense, physical AI and infrastructure mechanisms do not replace that center of gravity. The existing distinction between Trump’s exact words and Huang’s endorsement remains.
- Dario gains concrete military-use and open-weight boundaries. The September pacing position remains controlling when earlier writing is less restrictive.
- Andrew gains uneven task acceleration, validation and coordination bottlenecks rather than generic optimism.
- Hinton now explicitly reflects his September 16 support for slowing superintelligence and his criticism of kill-switch reassurance.
- Eliezer gains the book thesis, irretrievability mechanism and controlled argumentative voice. No numerical P(doom) or new timing claim was invented.

## Limitations and date checks

- Publication dates for Altman’s Abundant Intelligence and Sora 2 essays were not recoverable from the accessible pages. They are intentionally undated; neither counts as evidence of a September 2026 change.
- Andrew Ng’s generalists article body and dated issue tag say April 17; one aggregate category page says April 20. The catalog uses the article body’s April 17 date.
- LeWorldModel was first submitted March 13 and revised June 3, 2026. The catalog uses the original submission date; the abstract read was the current version.
- The WEF [Radio Davos transcript](https://www.weforum.org/podcasts/radio-davos/episodes/ai-agi-dario-amodei-demis-hassabis/) was used to keep Amodei and Hassabis’s statements separate. The January 20 date comes from the official video, not the later podcast listing.
- The NVIDIA CES page says January 5 and notes a September product-name edit. It is not presented as a newly delivered September keynote.
- The TIME Hinton extraction included unrelated metadata before the byline. Only the attributed December 11 essay body was used.
- Corporate economics, forecaster self-grades and policy proposals are attributed positions, not independently verified findings. Book/episode descriptions and paper abstracts are explicitly labeled so future participants cannot treat them as fully reviewed long-form sources.
- Existing saved runs still reflect their original generation context. A new source list is not evidence that old answers were produced from it.
