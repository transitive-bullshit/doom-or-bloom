## Evidence and assessment boundaries

- Original required URLs remain attached to each snapshot and intake record. A mapping to a related accessible version does not claim that the exact blocked original was read. The four partially accessible records retain `partial` status and remain required-source completion blockers.
- Canaries' 19% employment shortfall and Jagged Frontier's 19-percentage-point case-correctness reduction have different denominators, outcomes and study designs. Neither becomes a generic AI penalty or national unemployment rate.
- Occupational exposure and observed chatbot adoption do not randomize AI treatment. Randomized task or access studies have causal interpretations within their assigned conditions; they do not establish universal productivity, durable team replacement or economy-wide effects.
- The Cybernetic Teammate's 791 journal completers include 776 complete surveys. Do not merge these counts with the NBER abstract or infer a new sample merely from the difference.
- Copilot assignment ITT and instrumented-use IV/LATE estimates are distinct. Telemetry-based application time does not measure all paid hours, output quality or aggregate productivity.
- US young-worker hiring and Danish earnings/hours can differ without factual contradiction. The assessment should ask which population, period, outcome and mechanism the participant means before treating a disagreement as an error or tension.
- The indicators hub and Canaries share data; related publications can share authors, samples or mechanisms. Cross-links do not multiply independent evidence or create viewpoint-based scoring priors.
- Recent publication dates do not refresh older interventions or evidence windows. All six assets remain draft; no individual review, activation or release freeze is implied.
