## Comparison and evidence boundaries

- Rapid research acceleration, institutionally mediated adoption and century-scale stakes are different attributed scenarios. Compare definitions, mechanisms and horizons rather than assign quality from the author's conclusion.
- A model recognizing its evaluation context is distinct from the forecast series named Situational Awareness. Capability development, adoption, control and catastrophic endpoints are separate judgments.
- Practical alignment pessimism is not impossibility in principle. Instrumental-drive arguments require goal-pursuit/planning assumptions; proxy optimization, intentional misuse, learned optimization and systemic erosion remain distinct.
- Ord's dated roughly one-in-ten unaligned-AI estimate concerns 100 years and includes permanent loss of potential in existential risk. Do not transfer it to a shorter horizon, ordinary harms or a current consensus.
- Hubs are discovery/publication identities with named examples. Their archives were not comprehensively read, and author/interviewee/quoted-source positions are not interchangeable. Empty homepage extraction and partial-series reading remain explicit on four records.
- Practitioner security work with human review is a specific account, not a controlled estimate of net AI security benefit. Organizational advice and incident retellings are not independently verified causal or forensic evidence.
- Historical theory, recent named examples and source inspection dates have different jobs. Topic metadata describes actual subject matter rather than tagging all entries as cyber or capability evaluations.
- Disclosures are attributed context, not evidence of dishonesty or neutrality. Related IDs respect current release dependencies; existing older artifacts retain their identity until deliberate compatible-release selection.
