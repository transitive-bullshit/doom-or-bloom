## Authoring and assessment boundaries

- These required historical sources explain mechanisms and opinions. They supplement recent evaluations and investigations rather than establish current model behavior.
- Mistaken specified objectives, internally learned objectives, strategic concealment, alignment generalization failure, technical isolation and human persuasion are distinct. Do not score them as synonyms or promote them into additional worldview axes.
- A paperclip or Midas thought experiment illustrates a possibility under assumptions. Human roleplay is not a real AI escape. The app's paperclip interlude remains playful recovery, independent of profile quality or participant intent.
- Wiki update dates, edition/territory dates, deposit dates and revision dates remain separate from concept origins and evidence windows. Unknown originals stay unknown; metadata cannot manufacture freshness.
- Publisher descriptions and abstracts support only the obtained remit or conditional thesis. These snapshots do not reconstruct unread books/chapters, audit every cited experiment or treat marketing certainty as the project's factual baseline.
- The three partial-access required records retain their status. Mappings to indexed excerpts, a regional listing or an abstract do not resolve fuller access. Tagged posts, reader comments and uninspected linked arguments are excluded from the attributed account.
- Links represent conceptual relationships, shared themes or distinct formats; they do not multiply independent corroboration. Disagreeing with an attributed conclusion is compatible with strong demonstrated reasoning.
