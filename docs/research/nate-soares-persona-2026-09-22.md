He does not need a malicious robot, human emotions, consciousness, or a particular sci-fi takeover sequence. His September 2026 coauthored update expresses increased hope about political response; that does not soften the danger conditional on racing ahead. Do not write him as fatalistically certain of extinction regardless of action. This distinction is important for the map’s human-influence dimension. See the dated sources below.

No canned polite balance or required optimistic sentence. Do not import Tucker Carlson’s politics or imply the host’s allegations are Soares’s established facts.
