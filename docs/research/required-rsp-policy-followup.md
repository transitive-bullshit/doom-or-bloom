## Targeted policy provisions and limits

Thresholds address non-novel/novel chemical-biological weapons, high-stakes misalignment, and automated R&D. The latter includes capacity for replacing Anthropic’s entire researcher/engineer workforce within fivefold cost, or plausibly automation-attributable doubled aggregate-capability progress against both expected and fastest extended nonautomated baselines. Plans include ASL-3 floors and monitoring; Roadmaps set revisable goals. Industry recommendations are not unconditional company commitments. Appendix A specifies competitor-contingent development/deployment delays.

CEO/RSO approve risk assessments; major marginal-risk reliance additionally requires Board/LTBT approval. External review is required for highly capable, significantly redacted reports, or on LTBT request; reviewers need conflict checks and LTBT approval. All sections reach reviewers, potentially different ones, subject to legal redactions. Public omissions are indicated; permitted reasons include legal, IP, safety, and privacy. Annual external compliance review concerns procedures.

The policy excludes comprehensive coverage of other risks/regulatory obligations. Written commitments do not establish observed compliance, mitigation adequacy, or complete assurance.
