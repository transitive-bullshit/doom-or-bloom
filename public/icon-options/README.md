# Doom or Bloom icon options

Eight original SVG concepts, each on a 64 × 64 viewBox with an opaque dark tile for contrast across browser themes. No fonts, external assets, or raster images in the icons.

Open `index.html` for individual downloads and previews at 16, 24, 32, and 48 pixels. `contact-sheet.png` and `contact-sheet.svg` show all eight options.

- **01 Eclipse** — Two futures, one world. (`01-eclipse.svg`)
- **02 Fork in the future** — A shared starting point. Diverging possibilities. (`02-fork.svg`)
- **03 Worldview compass** — Find your position without losing the bigger picture. (`03-worldview.svg`)
- **04 Paradox flower** — Sharp edges and soft petals share the same center. (`04-paradox-flower.svg`)
- **05 Horizon** — An open future coming into view. (`05-horizon.svg`)
- **06 D / B** — A compact monogram with a shared spine. (`06-db.svg`)
- **07 Open question** — A conversation that makes room for mixed views. (`07-dialogue.svg`)
- **08 Seed of uncertainty** — A small question with far-reaching possibilities. (`08-seed.svg`)

These are selection candidates; the application favicon has not been changed.
